References
==========

The following references provide relevant standards, frameworks, principles,
and technical resources that inform or complement the Human and Organizational
Readiness Framework for Trusted Agentic AI (HORA-TAI).

The references are provided for context and interoperability. Inclusion of a
reference does not imply that HORA-TAI is equivalent to, formally endorsed by,
or incorporated into the referenced standard or framework.

ITU-T and FG-TIDA
-----------------

[1] ITU-T Focus Group on Trust and Identity for Humans and Agentic AI
(FG-TIDA), "Focus Group on Trust and Identity for Humans and Agentic AI",
International Telecommunication Union.

The FG-TIDA work addresses trust management and interoperable digital identity
infrastructure for humans and agentic AI. HORA-TAI complements this work by
addressing the human and organizational conditions surrounding delegated
authority.

https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-T/focusgroups/tida/Pages/default.aspx

NIST AI Risk Management Framework
---------------------------------

[2] Tabassi, E. (2023), "Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework
(AI RMF 1.0)", NIST AI 100-1, National Institute of Standards and Technology.

The NIST AI RMF provides a voluntary framework for organizations designing,
developing, deploying, or using AI systems to manage AI risks and incorporate
trustworthiness considerations throughout the AI lifecycle.

https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.AI.100-1

[3] National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST), "NIST AI RMF
Playbook".

The AI RMF Playbook provides suggested actions and documentation practices
associated with the Govern, Map, Measure, and Manage functions of the NIST AI
RMF.

https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework/nist-ai-rmf-playbook

[4] Autio, C., et al. (2024), "Artificial Intelligence Risk Management
Framework: Generative Artificial Intelligence Profile", NIST AI 600-1,
National Institute of Standards and Technology.

The Generative AI Profile identifies risks and suggested risk-management
actions associated with generative AI and complements the broader NIST AI RMF.

https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.AI.600-1

ISO and IEC
-----------

[5] ISO/IEC 42001:2023, "Information technology - Artificial intelligence -
Management system", International Organization for Standardization and
International Electrotechnical Commission.

ISO/IEC 42001 specifies requirements for establishing, implementing,
maintaining, and continually improving an Artificial Intelligence Management
System (AIMS) within an organization.

https://www.iso.org/standard/42001.html

[6] ISO/IEC 23894:2023, "Information technology - Artificial intelligence -
Guidance on risk management", International Organization for Standardization
and International Electrotechnical Commission.

This standard provides guidance for organizations that develop, produce,
deploy, or use AI systems to manage risks specifically related to AI.

https://www.iso.org/standard/77304.html

[7] ISO/IEC 22989:2022, "Information technology - Artificial intelligence -
Artificial intelligence concepts and terminology", International Organization
for Standardization and International Electrotechnical Commission.

This standard provides concepts and terminology for the field of artificial
intelligence and supports a common vocabulary for AI-related activities.

https://www.iso.org/standard/74296.html

OECD AI Principles
------------------

[8] OECD (2024), "OECD AI Principles", Organisation for Economic Co-operation
and Development.

The OECD AI Principles provide internationally agreed principles for
trustworthy and human-centric AI, including human-centred values and
fairness, transparency and explainability, robustness, security and safety,
and accountability.

https://oecd.ai/en/ai-principles

[9] OECD (2024), "Recommendation of the Council on Artificial Intelligence",
OECD Legal Instruments.

The Recommendation provides principles and policy recommendations for
responsible stewardship of trustworthy AI.

https://legalinstruments.oecd.org/en/instruments/OECD-LEGAL-0449

AI Governance and Human Oversight
---------------------------------

[10] European Union, "Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 of the European Parliament
and of the Council of 13 June 2024 laying down harmonised rules on artificial
intelligence and amending certain Union legislative acts (Artificial
Intelligence Act)".

The EU AI Act establishes a risk-based regulatory framework for AI and includes
requirements concerning human oversight for certain AI systems.

https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj

[11] UNESCO (2021), "Recommendation on the Ethics of Artificial Intelligence",
United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization.

The Recommendation provides a global normative framework addressing human
rights, human agency, governance, transparency, accountability, and related
ethical considerations in AI.

https://www.unesco.org/en/legal-affairs/recommendation-ethics-artificial-intelligence

Trustworthy and Responsible AI
------------------------------

[12] National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST), "Artificial
Intelligence Risk Management Framework".

The AI RMF provides a voluntary, use-case-agnostic framework for managing AI
risks and incorporating trustworthiness considerations into AI design,
development, deployment, use, and evaluation.

https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework

[13] OECD, "AI Principles".

The OECD AI Principles provide an international reference point for
human-centred and trustworthy AI and emphasize accountability and human
oversight-related considerations.

https://www.oecd.org/en/topics/ai-principles.html

Agentic AI and Delegated Authority
----------------------------------

[14] ITU-T Focus Group on Trust and Identity for Humans and Agentic AI
(FG-TIDA), "Draft Technical Specification and related working documents",
International Telecommunication Union.

FG-TIDA provides the principal standards-development context for HORA-TAI,
particularly in relation to trust management, identity, authorization, and
interactions between humans and agentic AI.

https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-T/focusgroups/tida/Pages/default.aspx

[15] NIST AI Resource Center, "AI Risk Management Framework Resources",
National Institute of Standards and Technology.

The NIST AI Resource Center provides implementation resources supporting the
operationalization of AI risk management, including testing, evaluation,
verification, and validation activities.

https://airc.nist.gov/

Relationship to HORA-TAI
------------------------

The references above inform different aspects of the HORA-TAI framework.

FG-TIDA provides the immediate standards-development context concerning trust
and identity for humans and agentic AI.

NIST AI RMF provides a risk-management framework that complements the HORA-TAI
focus on human and organizational readiness.

ISO/IEC 42001 provides an organizational management-system perspective that
supports the HORA-TAI emphasis on governance, organizational capability, and
continuous improvement.

ISO/IEC 23894 provides AI risk-management guidance relevant to assessing the
risks associated with delegated AI activity.

ISO/IEC 22989 provides a broader AI terminology foundation.

The OECD AI Principles and UNESCO Recommendation provide international
references for human-centred, accountable, and trustworthy AI.

The EU AI Act provides a regulatory reference for human oversight and
risk-based governance within the European Union.

HORA-TAI is intended to complement these approaches by focusing specifically on
the readiness of humans and organizations to delegate defined authority to
agentic AI systems.
